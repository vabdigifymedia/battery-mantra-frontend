## Brand & Style

This design system drives an urgent, high-trust automotive commerce experience built for immediate consumer action and rapid conversion. The identity synthesizes the speed and micro-urgency of hyper-delivery applications (Zepto) with the certified structural authority of modern automotive resale platforms (Spinny) and the restrained precision of modern Apple mobile interfaces.

### Personality & Tone
- **Electric & Immediate:** Instills confidence that roadside assistance and battery replacement are minutes away.
- **Authoritative & Certified:** Eradicates the suspicion typically associated with unorganized aftermarket automotive repairs through clean engineering metrics and crisp badge iconography.
- **Frictionless Utility:** Minimizes mental overhead during emergency vehicle breakdowns using clear visual affordances, high-visibility pricing, and transparent trade-in calculations.

### Visual Architecture
The interface combines structural minimalism with tactful, high-density transactional cues. Light tactile surfaces, feather-light containment borders (`#e2e8f0`), soft contextual glows, and pronounced squircle curvatures (`1rem` to `1.5rem`) establish an organized visual hierarchy. High-energy crimson anchors primary decision pathways, while emerald and amber micro-surfaces immediately communicate warranties, express timelines, and monetary exchange trade-offs.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for tactical visual hierarchy, combining energetic conversion triggers with institutional dark slates and hyper-clean surfaces.

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Primary — Energetic Crimson (`#dc2626` / `#e11d48`):** The primary kinetic driver. Reserved strictly for conversion anchors: primary checkout triggers, urgent roadside dispatch actions, active progress steppers, and real-time battery status markers.
- **Secondary — Deep Slate Canvas (`#0f172a`):** Supplies structural gravitas. Applied to high-emphasis typography, vehicle identification cards, bottom navigation backdrops, and active bottom sheets.
- **Tertiary — Assurance Emerald (`#10b981`):** Communicates OEM guarantee validity, free 60-minute express doorstep fitment badges, and authentic manufacturer warranty tokens.
- **Accent — Monetary Amber (`#f59e0b`):** Drives scrap/old-battery exchange deduction metrics, instant cash rebates, and price-cut callouts.
- **Neutral Core — Soft Slate & Surface Gray:**
  - Base Background: Pure White (`#ffffff`) to Soft Surface Gray (`#f8fafc`).
  - Card & Container Fill: Light Tinted Slate (`#ffffff` resting on `#f1f5f9`).
  - Subtle Dividing Line: Neutral Border (`#e2e8f0`).
  - Secondary/Caption Text: Cool Neutral (`#64748b`).

### Color Usage Rules
- Never use crimson for non-interactive decorative elements. Crimson must signal direct interaction, vital alert status, or primary cart conversion.
- Pair Emerald and Amber strictly with high-contrast text or tint-matched 10% opacity pill backgrounds (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)` and `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`).
- Critical system states (failed battery diagnostic, out of stock) utilize Crimson, while regular vehicle health checkpoints use Emerald.

## Typography

The type system is powered entirely by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, providing a modern, geometric balance that pairs rounded approachability with tech-forward clarity.

### Typographic Principles
- **Aggressive Weight Contrast:** Large, tight-tracked geometric bold weights (`800` and `700`) are used for numbers, warranty badges, delivery estimations, and car model selectors to support rapid mobile scanning.
- **Tabular Numerics for Real-Time Pricing:** All currency symbols (₹), base rates, scrap discount strikes, and net checkout amounts must employ tabular numbers (`tnum`) to eliminate layout shifts during trade-in toggle selections.
- **Tight Micro-Labels:** Badges and metric callouts leverage uppercase tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) at sizes between `10px` and `12px` with extra-bold weighting for legibility against colored pill fills.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout enforces a mobile-first philosophy optimized for quick vehicle selection, model compatibility matching, and checkout flows.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Mobile Grid (Primary Form Factor):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer margins and a `1rem` column gutter. Touch targets remain strict at `>= 48px` vertically.
- **Tablet / Desktop Extension:** Clamped to a max-width container of `1120px`. The desktop view translates into an 8-to-12 column architecture, placing the vehicle selection pane and battery diagnostics sticky on the left, with product recommendation carousels and exchange breakdowns on the right.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Dense 4px baseline rhythm (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 12px, `space-lg` = 16px, `space-xl` = 24px, `space-2xl` = 32px).
- **Safe Area Insets:** Fixed bottom action bar (Sticky CTA) incorporates dynamic system bottom bar paddings with an interior padding of `1rem` around the primary checkout triggers.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, instead using layered surface cards, tactile borders, and targeted ambient glows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#f8fafc`. Completely flat, non-elevated ground surface.
- **Level 1 (Interactive Cards & Catalog Units):** Pure White (`#ffffff`) background bordered with `1px solid #f1f5f9`. Micro-shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Selections & Cart Summaries):** White (`#ffffff`) background surrounded by `1px solid #e2e8f0`. Shadow: `0 6px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Bars, Drawers, Modals):** White (`#ffffff`) with subtle upper border `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Shadow: `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.
- **Dynamic Colored Micro-Glow (Primary CTA):** Crimson Primary Button incorporates a targeted button glow: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.35)`.
- **Warranty/Express Badge Elevation:** Flat surfaces with high-contrast pastel fills (`#ecfdf5` for Emerald, `#fffbeb` for Amber) combined with hairline interior stroke overlays (`1px inset rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`).

## Shapes

The shape system employs squircle-style curvatures aligned with modern Apple iOS and contemporary Indian consumer tech interfaces.

### Curvature Matrix
- **Standard Structural Cards:** `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / 16px) creates a balanced, pocketable container for battery specs, delivery estimations, and trade-in pricing tables.
- **Hero Containers & Bottom Drawers:** `rounded-3xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) for vehicle configuration selectors, roadside breakdown bottom sheets, and promotional hero highlights.
- **Buttons & Form Inputs:** Uniform `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px) to `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / 16px) providing friendly, finger-accessible tap zones.
- **Badges, Filters & Chips:** Fully curved pill shapes (`9999px`) for guarantee badges, scrap trade-in indicators, and battery AH rating tags.

## Components

### 1. Primary & Secondary Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Order & Fit in 60 Mins"):** Crimson solid fill (`#dc2626`), high-contrast white text (`#ffffff`), `rounded-2xl`, height `54px`, `label-lg` weighting. Features an ambient red under-glow. Active press scale effect: `scale(0.98)`.
- **Secondary Action ("Check Compatibility"):** Slate solid (`#0f172a`) or clean ghost outline (`1.5px solid #e2e8f0` on `#ffffff`), label color `#0f172a`, `rounded-2xl`, height `48px`.

### 2. Battery Product Card
- Encased in a pure white background with `rounded-2xl` radius, bordered by `1px solid #f1f5f9`.
- **Header Slot:** Car model compatibility pill (`#f1f5f9`, `#334155`) paired with an Emerald express tag (`"60-min Express Delivery"`).
- **Middle Section:** Left side features the battery silhouette with brand badging (Amaron/Exide); right side highlights technical specifications (Ah capacity, warranty duration, terminal type) in tabular typography.
- **Footer Slot:** Net dynamic pricing stack showing the scrap battery exchange deduction in Amber (`"Save ₹1,200 with Old Battery Exchange"`) next to the crimson add-to-cart/book-now button.

### 3. Guarantee & Feature Badges (Chips)
- **Express Fitment Badge:** Emerald fill (`#ecfdf5`), border (`#a7f3d0`), text (`#065f46`), featuring a bolt or clock icon.
- **Exchange Rebate Tag:** Amber fill (`#fffbeb`), border (`#fde68a`), text (`#92400e`), pill-shaped with bold currency figures.
- **Warranty Tag:** Deep Slate fill (`#0f172a`), crisp white text, detailing replacement vs. pro-rata guarantee splits (e.g., `"36+36 Months OEM Warranty"`).

### 4. Interactive Vehicle Selector (Dropdown/Modal)
- High-touch surface featuring Make > Model > Fuel Variant dropdown pills.
- Active selected state triggers a subtle Deep Slate border (`2px solid #0f172a`) with a soft blue-slate glow, guaranteeing zero vehicle mismatch errors before product recommendations render.

### 5. Scrap Exchange Toggle (Custom Component)
- High-conversion switch card showing: *"Exchange Old Battery & Get Instant Cash Off"*.
- Unchecked state: Clean neutral border (`#e2e8f0`).
- Checked state: Tinted Amber background (`#fffbeb`), border (`#f59e0b`), with a dynamic checkout price reduction counter and checkmark badge.

### 6. Form Fields & Search Inputs
- **Base Style:** Background `#f8fafc`, border `1.5px solid #e2e8f0`, radius `rounded-xl`, padding `14px 16px`. Focus state transitions border to `#dc2626` with an inner shadow ring `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.1)`. Placeholder text in `#94a3b8`.
